# Third-party acknowledgements and redistribution review

KeyMind includes derived word lists that may be subject to their original license and attribution requirements.

| File/resource | Reported upstream source | Licensing information to verify before redistribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data/en-frequency.txt` | [TextBlob](https://github.com/sloria/TextBlob) English spelling/frequency resources | TextBlob is MIT licensed; confirm the source-data history and applicable notice requirements. |
| `data/en-extended.txt` | [CMU Pronouncing Dictionary (CMUDict)](https://github.com/cmusphinx/cmudict) word forms | CMUDict BSD-style terms; preserve the relevant original notice. |
| Supplemental Arabic stop words | [spaCy](https://github.com/explosion/spaCy) Arabic resources | spaCy is MIT licensed; verify that the precise data source and version are covered. |
| Curated word lists | App-specific additions | Verify ownership and permission to publish. |

These upstream attributions were provided with the original project. The current package does **not** independently establish the provenance of each entry or include a full license audit. Before publishing this repository or releasing packaged builds, review exact upstream versions and include required notices and license texts for every redistributed dataset.
